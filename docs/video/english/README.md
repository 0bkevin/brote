# English walkthrough sources

Real browser recordings and screenshots from Brote's English web interface. The capture viewport was 1280 × 720; recordings retain the 2560 × 1440 display pixels. The two scene manifests select actions from this material, trim waiting time, and preserve the full frame.

The edited tour uses fictional demo profiles. Noah's recorded ledger ends at 108 practice Brotes: 100 allocated, 20 saved, two approved purchases of 5 each, and mission rewards of 12, 16, and 10. His saved goal remains at 20 Brotes. Different chapters demonstrate features rather than every ledger entry.

The local Solana session uses a disposable testnet guardian. The film fills transfer fields and cancels deposit review before signing. Its Explorer receipt shows an earlier verified testnet transfer, described in [testnet QA](../../TESTNET_QA.md). No keys or new signed transfers are included in these assets.

`sources.json` records each capture's size and SHA-256. Brote artwork follows the repository's MIT license. Outfit and Nunito Sans retain their included SIL Open Font licenses. Narration is generated at render time; the original music is regenerated with `scripts/make-walkthrough-music.py` and its WAV is not tracked. See [rebuild instructions](../../VIDEO_GUIDE.md).
